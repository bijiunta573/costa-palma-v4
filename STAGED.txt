Game:        "Costa Palma" — an original sun-soaked coastal city. Leo
             Marchetti, a wheelman, runs three jobs for fixer Mama Rosa.
             Nothing is taken from any commercial game: own city, names,
             story, art (procedural), sounds (synthesized).

Entrypoint:  main.ts is authored for this entry (stages game/ and
             screenshots/ into the output dir, writes dossier.json via
             buildDossier from vendor/makefun-game.ts with the gate's
             caps). serve.ts, shots.json, LICENSE and
             vendor/makefun-game.ts are the kit's, byte-identical.

KNOWN WEAKNESSES
  - Frame time is clamped at 50 ms, so machines under ~20 fps run the
    game in slow motion instead of dropping steps (visible in headless
    SwiftShader; fine on real hardware).
  - Car collision is a single circle per car: long vehicles can clip a
    building corner at glancing angles; police steering can nose into a
    wall briefly before the next frame's push-out frees it.
  - Pedestrians keep to sidewalk lines and the marina apron edge; they
    do not use crosswalks as such, and gunmen fight in the open without
    taking cover.
  - The sea is one scrolling texture with no waves or reflections, and
    boat hulls are simple boxes; the marina reads as dressing, not a sim.
